PRODUCT CATEGORIES
================================================================================

1. SHAPED REFRACTORIES (Pre-formed Bricks)
   
   1.1 Fireclay Bricks
       - Al2O3 Content: 25-45%
       - Temperature Range: 1200-1500°C
       - Applications: Boiler settings, furnace linings, backup linings
       - Grades: STD-30, STD-40, HFK-28, HFK-32, HFK-36
       - Properties: Good thermal shock resistance, economical
   
   1.2 High Alumina Bricks
       - Al2O3 Content: 45-99%
       - Temperature Range: 1550-1850°C
       - Applications: Steel ladles, cement kilns, glass furnaces
       - Grades: HA-45, HA-60, HA-70, HA-80, HA-90
       - Properties: Excellent thermal stability, corrosion resistance
   
   1.3 Insulating Fire Bricks (IFB)
       - Density: 0.5-1.2 g/cc
       - Temperature Range: 1260-1600°C
       - Applications: Backup linings, kiln insulation
       - Grades: IFB-23, IFB-26, IFB-28, IFB-30, IFB-32
       - Properties: Low thermal conductivity, energy saving
   
   1.4 Basic Bricks
       - MgO Content: 70-95%
       - Temperature Range: 1600-1800°C
       - Applications: Cement kiln burning zones, steel ladles
       - Types: Magnesia, Magnesia-Chrome, Magnesia-Spinel
       - Properties: Excellent slag resistance
   
   1.5 Silica Bricks
       - SiO2 Content: >95%
       - Temperature Range: Up to 1650°C
       - Applications: Glass furnaces, coke ovens
       - Properties: High load bearing capacity

2. UNSHAPED REFRACTORIES (Monolithic)
   
   2.1 Conventional Castables
       - Cement Content: 10-25%
       - Applications: General furnace linings
       - Installation: Casting, vibration
   
   2.2 Low Cement Castables (LCC)
       - Cement Content: 5-8%
       - Applications: High-wear areas, steel ladles
       - Properties: Superior strength, low porosity
   
   2.3 Ultra Low Cement Castables (ULCC)
       - Cement Content: <3%
       - Applications: Critical linings, flow control
       - Properties: Excellent high-temperature properties
   
   2.4 No-Cement Castables
       - Binder: Colloidal silica, alumina gel
       - Applications: Ultra-high temperature areas
       - Properties: No low-melting phases
   
   2.5 Mortars
       - Types: Air-setting, Heat-setting
       - Applications: Brick jointing, repairs
   
   2.6 Ramming Masses
       - Applications: Induction furnaces, troughs
       - Installation: Ramming
   
   2.7 Gunning Mixes
       - Applications: Hot repairs, maintenance
       - Installation: Gunning/spraying

3. CERAMIC FIBER PRODUCTS
   
   3.1 Ceramic Fiber Blankets
       - Temperature: 1260-1430°C
       - Density: 64-160 kg/m³
       - Applications: Furnace insulation, expansion joints
   
   3.2 Ceramic Fiber Modules
       - Pre-fabricated anchor systems
       - Quick installation
       - Applications: Furnace linings
   
   3.3 Ceramic Fiber Boards
       - Rigid insulation panels
       - Temperature: 1260-1430°C
       - Applications: Backup insulation
   
   3.4 Ceramic Fiber Papers
       - Applications: Gasket material, expansion joints

4. FLOW CONTROL SYSTEMS
   
   4.1 Slide Gate Systems
       - Types: 1-plate, 2-plate
       - Applications: Steel ladles, tundishes
       - Materials: Alumina-carbon, alumina-zirconia-carbon
   
   4.2 Nozzles & SEN (Submerged Entry Nozzles)
       - Materials: Alumina-carbon, zirconia
       - Applications: Continuous casting
   
   4.3 Stopper Rods
       - Applications: Ladle to tundish flow control

5. ACID PROOF MATERIALS
   
   5.1 Acid Proof Bricks
       - Applications: Chemical plants, pickling tanks
       - Properties: Resistant to acids
   
   5.2 Acid Proof Tiles
       - Applications: Flooring, tank linings
   
   5.3 Carbon Bricks
       - Applications: HF exposure, chemical vessels
       - Properties: Resistant to hydrofluoric acid
