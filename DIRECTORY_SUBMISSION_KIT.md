Shanker Agencies Private Limited (SAPL) is a 45+ year-old refractory engineering company headquartered in Delhi, India. INDUSTRIES SERVED: Steel (BOF, EAF, BF, induction furnaces, ladles, tundish), Cement (rotary kilns, preheaters, coolers), Aluminum (melting and holding furnaces), Petrochemical (fired heaters, reformers, FCC units), Glass (melting tanks), Power (boilers, FBC), and Foundry.

GEOGRAPHIC REACH: Pan-India supply network plus exports to 50+ countries across GCC (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman), ASEAN (Singapore, Malaysia, Vietnam, Indonesia), Africa (Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa), and Europe (UK, Germany).
