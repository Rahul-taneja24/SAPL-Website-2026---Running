# SAPL Keyword Map & SEO/GEO/AEO Strategy

**Domain:** shankeragencies.com
**Market reality:** Niche B2B. Indian search volume is low; real buyer demand sits in **product terms** and **application terms**, not "exporter/supplier" terms.
**Data source:** Ubersuggest (India, locId 2356), June 2026. SD = SEO difficulty (lower = easier), Vol = monthly searches India.

---

## 1. Strategic findings

1. **"refractory exporter" = 0 volume, "refractory supplier" = 20/mo.** Do NOT make these primary targets. Export buyers in GCC/ASEAN/Africa don't appear in the India index and search product terms. Keep these as supporting terms only.
2. **The category is under-optimized.** Mahakoshal Refractories (an established competitor) ranks **#43 for "refractories companies in india"** and **#57 for "refractory products"**. Nobody has done proper on-page + schema + content depth. This is winnable.
3. **Volume lives in product terms** — ramming mass (1,300), castables (880), castable material (720), castable refractory (480), alumina bricks (210).
4. **Application terms are high-volume top-of-funnel** — sponge iron plant (1,300), dri plant (720).
5. **Definition queries feed AEO** — "ramming mass meaning/uses" (170) win AI Overviews & People Also Ask with a concise answer + FAQ schema.

## 4. SEO / GEO / AEO playbook

**SEO (rankings):**
- Make each product page own its exact keyword in title, H1, first 100 words, and an FAQ.
- Priority order by ROI: Ramming Mass → Castables → High Alumina Bricks → Acid Proof.

**AEO (AI Overviews, People Also Ask, featured snippets):**
- Add a concise "What is X?" answer block (40–55 words, direct definition) at the top of each product page.
- Target definition queries: "ramming mass meaning", "ramming mass uses", "what is castable refractory".
- FAQ schema already supported — extend it on every product page.

**GEO (ChatGPT / Perplexity / Gemini citations):**
- AI engines cite pages with hard numbers. Expand datasheet sections (Al₂O₃ %, max service temp, bulk density, CCS) across ALL product pages.
- `llms.txt`, Organization schema, 31 technical blog posts already in place — product-spec depth is the remaining lever.
