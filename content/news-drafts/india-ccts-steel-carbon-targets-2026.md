### Summary
India's environment ministry has issued a draft notification assigning CO2-intensity reduction
targets to 255 iron and steel plants under the Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS), starting
FY2026-27. The 255 units carry a combined baseline of 358.6 MtCO2e; India's average emission
intensity (2.54 tCO2/tonne crude steel) still runs well above the global average (1.9 tCO2/tonne)
and the Ministry of Steel's 2030 goal of 2.2 tCO2/tonne. Required cuts range 2.1–9.3% per entity
(median ~5.5%), with steeper reductions assigned to higher-emitting plants.

### Why it matters (direct answer)
For refractory and insulation buyers, this is a compliance clock, not a market abstraction: a
Centre for Science and Environment analyst quoted in the coverage names energy efficiency — not
new capital-intensive process routes — as the first lever plants will pull to hit FY2026-27
targets. Furnace lining and insulation selection is squarely inside that lever.
