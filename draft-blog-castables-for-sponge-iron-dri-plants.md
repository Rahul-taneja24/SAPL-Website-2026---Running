<h2>Why One Castable Spec Doesn't Fit the Whole Kiln</h2>
<p>A DRI rotary kiln processes iron ore, coal and dolomite through three thermal zones as it rotates: a preheating zone where the charge is dried and warmed, a reduction zone where the actual metallization reaction happens at 1,000-1,100°C under constant tumbling abrasion, and a discharge/cooler transition where hot DRI exits into the cooler. <h2>Planning Relining Around Two Different Clocks</h2>
<p>Because the reduction zone wears out roughly 2-3x faster than the preheating zone, campaign planning should track them separately rather than scheduling one kiln-wide shutdown for both.
